How do I choose between hydraulic and mechanical steering?

Mechanical steering is a cost-effective choice for smaller boats with engines up to around 55 HP. Above that — or if you want a lighter feel, a second helm station or autopilot — hydraulic steering is the better long-term investment.

Can I install a Navic steering system myself?

Mechanical systems and most hydraulic outboard kits are designed for DIY installation and include detailed instructions. Electronic power steering and joystick systems must be installed and commissioned by a certified Navic dealer.

How often should hydraulic fluid be checked?

Check the fluid level at the helm at the start of every season and after any service work. Use Navic hydraulic steering fluid or an approved equivalent, and bleed the system if steering feels spongy.

Are Navic systems compatible with my autopilot?

Our hydraulic systems accept most major autopilot pumps via standard fittings, and Vector electronic steering integrates natively over NMEA 2000.

What does the warranty cover?

All Navic steering products carry a 3-year limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, extended to 5 years when installed by a certified dealer.
